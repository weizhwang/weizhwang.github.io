My research lies at the intersection of applied probability and statistics, control and (stochastic) differential equations. I completed my bachelor degree in Fudan University. During my undergraduate, I was fortunate to be advised by [Prof. Yiping Lu](https://2prime.github.io/) and [Prof. Kaizheng Wang](https://kw2934.github.io/). I am also deeply grateful to [Prof. Yingzhou Li](https://yingzhouli.com/) for his generous guidance and support throughout my academic journey.
